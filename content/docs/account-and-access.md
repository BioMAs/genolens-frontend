# Your account and access

## Your profile

Open your **Profile** from the menu at the top right. It shows your email address, when your account was created and when you last signed in, as well as your plan, your quotas and your add-ons. **Sign out** is at the top of the page.

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email address** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link **within one hour**.
4. Choose a new password (at least 8 characters, typed twice).

If the page says *This reset link is no longer valid*, the link has expired or was already used: ask for a new one.

## When your access period ends

Some accounts have an access period with an end date, for instance a trial or a fixed-term subscription. You receive reminder emails **7, 3 and 1 days** before the end date.

After that date, your account switches to **read-only access**. A banner at the top of every page tells you so.

**You can still:**

- open all your projects and results;
- explore, filter and change thresholds;
- download your exports.

**You can no longer:**

- create projects;
- upload datasets;
- launch analyses.

Nothing is deleted. To renew, contact **support@scilicium.com**. Everything is available again as soon as your access is extended.

## Other account states

If your account needs attention, GenoLens shows a dedicated page instead of the app:

- **Subscription expired**: your subscription has ended. **Renew subscription** leads to the Pricing page.
- **Account suspended**: access has been suspended by an administrator. Use **Contact support**.
- **Activation pending**: your account has been created but is not active yet.

## Replaying the guided tour

Each main screen has a short guided tour, shown on your first visit. To see it again, open the command palette (**⌘K / Ctrl+K**) and choose *Replay the guided tour of this screen*.
