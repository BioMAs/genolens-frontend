# Plans, quotas and add-ons

## Where to see your plan

Your plan and your remaining quota appear in two places:

- the **Dashboard**, under **My plan**;
- your **Profile** (menu at the top right, or **⌘K / Ctrl+K → Profile & plan**), under **Usage & quotas** and **Subscription & Billing**.

The meters show **Analyses** (left this month), **Projects** (owned) and **AI credits**.

The exact limits of your plan are shown on your meters and on the **Pricing** page.

## How the analysis quota works

- **One analysis = one unit**, whatever its number of comparisons. An analysis with twelve comparisons costs the same as an analysis with one.
- **Only successful analyses count.** A failed analysis, or one that produced no comparison, is not charged.
- Analyses waiting in the queue are reserved: you cannot launch more than your remaining quota at once.
- **The quota resets on the 1st of each month.** Unused analyses do not carry over.
- You receive an email when you have used 80% of your monthly quota.

When the quota is used up, **Launch Analysis** is disabled until the next reset. Your existing results stay fully accessible. An analysis that was already running when you reached the limit still finishes.

> Exploring results, changing thresholds, running GSEA, exporting and generating reports never use the analysis quota.

## Add-ons

Add-ons are extra modules on top of your plan. An administrator enables them on your account.

| Add-on | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| **Scientific tools** | GSEA and leading edge, custom gene sets, signature score, contrast scatter, DEG patterns. |
| **Reporting** | Branded PDF reports (with a Pro or Enterprise plan). |
| **Drug Discovery** | Drug target ranking and matching of your comparisons against it. |
| **Skin claims** | Cosmetic claims from skin transcriptomics. |

Your **Profile** lists them under **Add-on modules**. A locked module shows a button to request access.

## Changing plan

Plans are changed by the GenoLens team. On the **Pricing** page, click the plan you want: this sends a pre-filled request, and the team gets back to you. From your profile, **Upgrade Plan** leads to the same page. **Manage Billing** appears there once you have a paid subscription.
